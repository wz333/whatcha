Words and pictures

A picture or word looks wrong. How do I tell you?

Tap the question mark at the top left of the word card (or beside the progress bar during a lesson) and choose what's wrong. One tap sends it to us. If the picture is wrong or inappropriate, the word also goes to the trash, so you won't study it again.

How do I stop studying a word?

Tap the trash can beside the progress bar. You have a few seconds to undo. To bring it back later, open My Words, choose the removed filter, open the word and put it back.

I already know a new word. Do I have to learn it?

No. Tap I know this on its card. Whatcha checks it again in about a month, and if you miss it then, it is studied like any other word.

When will I see a word again?

Whatcha schedules each review for around when you are about to forget the word. Words you know well come back less and less often, never more than six months apart. The quizzes get harder as you remember a word longer: first you pick it, later you say or spell it.

How do I change how many words I study a day?

Open Me and change New words a day and Reviews a day. These limits are for Daily Learning only. A category's own lessons have no limit.

Can I see the word in my language?

Yes, on the new-word card and the word card: tap the translate button beside the speaker. Choose the language under Translation Language on Me. Quizzes stay in English.

Speaking and listening

The app doesn't hear me when I say a word.

Check that Whatcha may use the microphone and speech recognition: open the iPhone's Settings, then Apps, then Whatcha. Speak after the sound that opens the microphone, in a quiet place. If it misses you twice, tap I said it.

Your voice is checked on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.

I can't hear the words or the sounds.

Whatcha plays at your media volume, so turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open. Sound effects can be turned on or off on Me. For a slower voice, turn on Slower voice there.

Whatcha Plus

What does Whatcha Plus include?

No ads, plus the speaking and spelling exercises. It is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

I bought Whatcha Plus but still see ads.

Open Me, tap Get Whatcha Plus, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On Me, tap Manage subscription. Or open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews; you keep Whatcha Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are asked for from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

How do I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Whatcha has no accounts; your progress is kept on your iPhone. Moving to a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it along. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Open Me and tap Reset progress at the bottom. This can't be undone.

How do I add the Quick Review widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle. Tap Edit, then Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap +). Search for Whatcha, choose a size and tap Add Widget.

How do I change the app's language?

Open Me and choose App Language: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, Español or 한국어. Lessons always stay in English.
